THE MOMENT
Leff, the huge tattooed half-orc, sits hunched over the table beside Brand, a mug lost in his massive hand, explaining with grave sincerity that in his warrior culture you must earn your last name in combat and be given it by a friend. He holds up one big fist and slowly closes it, the way he crushed a prison guard's skull. Brand, the old dwarf in his power armor, sits listening, moved, realising that the name he gave Leff in the prison, Skullcrusher, meant far more to him than Brand ever knew.
