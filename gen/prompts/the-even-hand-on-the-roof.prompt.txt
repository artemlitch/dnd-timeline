THE MOMENT
On the swaying carriage roof, The Even Hand stands calmly and tells Brand they are out of their depth: they are not his mission right now, and they should jump off. Mustang, the warforged police officer who has turned out to be one of The Even Hand's bodies, has just shoved Brand off the roof; to do it he had to switch off his own personality core, and his blue eyes have gone flat and dead. Brand, in his power armor, is going over the edge, and mechanical leg struts shoot out of his armor's legs and slam into the carriage's side and the road below, just catching him. Jyo crouches further along the roof, staring at the train's guns.
