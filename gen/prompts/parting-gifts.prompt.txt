{framing:wide}

SETTING
{setting:hobbstweedle/hobbstweedle-airfield}
In this moment: the Moonwatch, a sleek captured airship with a bound elemental ring glowing at its stern, waits on the grass with its gangway down, crates being carried aboard. Beside it the huge warship Kingsblade is readying to leave with the party's friends. Sentinels in black uniforms mill about saying goodbye. Time and light: afternoon.
{maps:Air Field Hobbstweedle}

THE MOMENT
At the foot of the Moonwatch's gangway the party receives its parting gifts, all at once. Eleanor, the engineer, presses a thick, hand-typed manual of the ship's upgrades into Brand's armored hands, and Clockwork, the warforged artificer, holds out a compact all-purpose tool to him. Next to them Eloria, the cleric, holds Sazed's bared forearm and traces a freshly inked tattoo of three glowing claw marks, the Wildheart Claw, while Sazed looks down at it. And Sheldor, the drow, closes Lirael's hand around the Bloodwell Vial, a small crystal vial of dark red blood on a chain.
