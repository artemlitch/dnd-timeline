THE MOMENT
Lirael stands in the war room doorway with his whole face plastered in cream pie from a gnome tripwire trap, cream dripping from his chin and his white hair, blinking through it. In front of him Brand and Paxon are nose to nose over Paxon's desk, mid-argument: Brand jabbing a thick finger at him, Paxon on his feet, pen still in hand, livid at being disturbed at work. Behind Lirael, two small gnome cooks peek around the door frame, grinning at their handiwork.
