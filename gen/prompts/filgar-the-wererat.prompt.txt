THE MOMENT
Sent down for a keg, Brand has instead settled in to get drunk with Filgar the wererat, a huge, scruffy rat-man who turns out to be completely relaxed. Brand sits on an upturned barrel in his power armor, red-faced, roaring with laughter, a tankard in his fist, his arm around Filgar's shoulders like an old friend. Filgar lounges on a keg beside him, tail curled, mug in his clawed hand. Screech, sent down after Brand, has given up on the keg too and is pouring himself a drink straight from the tap, and Sazed, the third one sent down, sits cross-legged passing round a smoking pipe of Larkleaf. On the stairs, Lirael is the only one still on task, hauling a keg up on his shoulder and ignoring their cheerful shouts to stay.
