THE MOMENT
Skye, a ten-year-old shifter girl, stands in the middle of the cabin, fists clenched, eyes wet and furious, shouting at Sazed. She has just learned she will be left at a hideout while he goes off to fight, and to her he is one more adult abandoning her to go and die. Sazed crouches in front of her, his hand half-raised toward her, with no good answer. Behind her the small children watch from the bunks, frightened.
