In this moment: the prison is burning, flames pouring from its windows. Up on the roof, prisoners and guards are trapped between the fire and the zombies. Time and light: night, lit by the burning prison.

THE MOMENT
The horde of zombies has caught Brand: he has gone down under a heap of grey, clawing bodies, and only one armored arm and his helmet still push up through the pile. Lirael has made it clear and stands near the gate, looking back in horror. Sazed, almost clear, has turned at a run to look back at Brand. Charging the other way into the horde come Rex, Leff and Rolick, to help; but Rolick has already been dragged down, zombies tearing at him, blood on the dirt.
