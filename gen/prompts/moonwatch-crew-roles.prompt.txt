THE MOMENT
The crew is lined up on the deck as the roles are handed out, each at or beside their new post. Screech, the new ship captain, stands in front of them all, hands behind his back, beaming with pride. Beezel, the helmsman, already has his hands on the ship's wheel, with Lirael, his co-helmsman, beside him at the controls. Brand, the ship's engineer, stands by the engine hatch with a toolbox. Sazed, communications and navigation officer, holds a rolled chart and a shuttered signal lamp. Behind them stand Sorenson, the red-bearded dwarf musician now head of security, and Erald, the elderly dwarf scribe now intelligence officer, clutching a stack of files.
