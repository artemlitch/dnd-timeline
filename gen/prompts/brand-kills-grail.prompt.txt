THE MOMENT
Brand, in his power armor, has just burst up out of the heap of zombies that buried him and driven his armored fist clean through Grail Grimdell's chest. The necromancer hangs on his arm, mouth open, staff falling. In that same instant every zombie in the yard drops: the whole horde is collapsing at once, bodies folding to the ground all around them like puppets with their strings cut. Lirael and Sazed, mid-fight at the edge of the yard, stare as their attackers fall.
