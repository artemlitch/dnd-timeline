THE MOMENT
Beezel, the halfling druid, has wild-shaped into a chipmunk and is scrambling through Sazed's precious books for fun, an old volume knocked open and pages crumpled under his tiny claws. Sazed, genuinely angry for once, his face hard and his spectacles askew, sweeps the chipmunk off the stack with the flat of his hand, sending it tumbling across the desk.
