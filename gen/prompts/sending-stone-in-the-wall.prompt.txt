THE MOMENT
Lirael has worked a loose rock out of the wall beside his bunk. In the hole behind it lie a small smooth stone and a crumpled picture of a goofy, soft-faced man. He holds the stone up in the lamplight, intrigued. Elarian, the young elf in the bunk above, leans down to look, and Tauren, the half-orc, watches from the bunk opposite.
