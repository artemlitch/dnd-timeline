THE MOMENT
Sazed stands in the aisle holding open an old book bound in bark and twine, its handmade paper rough and hand-inked. The whole shelf in front of him is lined with the same books, taken from shifter clans wiped out in the inquisition. His jaw is clenched and his knuckles are white on the cover, barely holding back his rage.
