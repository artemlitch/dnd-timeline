THE MOMENT
At the Moon Stones, Carrk, grief-stricken over the village he could not save, drapes a cloak around Sazed's shoulders as a parting gift: a Cloak of Displacement, its dark fabric shimmering so that Sazed's outline seems to waver and slide a hand's breadth off where he really stands. Two cups of Sylvan wine sit on a stone beside them. Carrk grips Sazed's arm, eyes wet.
