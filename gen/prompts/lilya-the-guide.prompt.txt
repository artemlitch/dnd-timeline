THE MOMENT
Lirael has just fitted the lodestone into his father's wayfinder compass, and it blazes with magical light in his hands. Rising from it is the apparition of a young drow girl, Lilya, translucent and glowing, floating above the compass and pointing the way into the dark. Lirael stares at her. Brand, in his power armor, holds up the lantern. Sazed watches the girl warily from the shadows.
