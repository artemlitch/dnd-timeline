THE MOMENT
Cromwell, very much alive after everyone thought he died in the prison, sits at the head of the table running a betting game with a single coin, flipping it high off his thumb. Lirael, Brand and Screech are betting against him. In front of Lirael sits a pile of winnings, and half of it is not real: those coins shimmer faintly, an illusion he conjured. Screech scoops up his own winnings, delighted. At the end of the table, Sheldor, a drow, and Erald, an old dwarf, stare at Cromwell in shock. Chester, Erald's pet mimic, a wooden treasure chest with a single eye, fangs and a long tongue, sidles up to Cromwell on stubby legs, nudging him and pushing its keyhole at him, begging to be lockpicked; Cromwell ignores it.
