THE MOMENT
The prisoner carriages have just rolled through DarkWood's gate. Through the bars of the carriage windows the new arrivals peer out at the walls: Lirael's face at one window, Brand's and Sazed's at another. All along the walls and the cell windows above, inmates hang on the bars chanting "fresh fish, fresh fish", fists banging. Guards in grey uniforms walk beside the carriages.
