THE MOMENT
The drill sergeant has told them no pranks, so they are planning one. Lirael, Brand and Sazed lean in over the table with Screech and three gnome cooks from the mansion, heads close together, voices low. Spingus, the gnome head chef, counts off the plan on his fingers: copy Paxon's prized portrait, draw all over the copy and hang it in the mansion's main hall. Dandy, the moustached gnome cook, grins wickedly; Fizbark, the third gnome, peers over his shoulder at the door to make sure no officer is listening. Screech is grinning into his mug.
