THE MOMENT
Fumble Figgins, a guard fleeing the massacre, hangs off a big alarm lever with both hands, and the prison's alarm bells ring out. All down the corridor cell doors burst open and prisoners pour out, fighting guards and each other. In the foreground Tauren lies wounded on the floor, and Scud bolts away down the corridor.
