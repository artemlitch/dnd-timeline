At the cemetery gate into upper Ardev, the mercenary captain Ellis springs his ambush. Ellis is beating the helpless Lirael bloody with his fists. Show it brutally: Lirael's face split and swollen, blood streaming from his nose and mouth, spattered across Ellis's knuckles, the grass and the nearest headstone. Ellis grins as he swings, enjoying it. Ellis's wizard stands clearly in view a few paces behind him, the second most prominent figure after Ellis and Lirael: a gaunt mercenary spellcaster in a long dark robe over a Dragons Claw leather harness, one arm thrust out toward Lirael, fingers splayed, his hand blazing with the same magical light as the bindings, and a visible stream of that crackling energy running from his palm to the bindings around Lirael. His face is lit from below by the spell, strained with concentration. Xera, a young female drow, is seized from behind by a mercenary, screaming and struggling, reaching toward Lirael.
