This scene is the campaign's grisliest horror: a demonic ritual in a slaughter room.

FRAMING
{framing:wide}

SETTING
{setting:mausoleum-of-alton-ardev/mausoleum-main-chamber}
In this moment: the dead hang around the walls on ropes and hooks. Blood runs down the carved murals and pools on the floor and into the blue fountain. In the middle of the room a stone table stands over the glowing demon portal set into the floor.
{maps:Mausoleum of Ardev}
Light: the blue glow of the fountain and the red-orange light boiling up from the portal.

THE MOMENT
Felicia Jr, Lirael's sister, an adult woman in her early twenties, lies tied unclothed to the stone table above the demon portal, in a trance, eyes glowing blank, runes burning on the stone around her. Her nudity is part of the story but must not be explicit: a torn length of cloth lies across her body, and her bindings and the swirling portal smoke cover the rest; nothing explicit is visible.
Fark, the town brothel-keeper revealed as a succubus, stands at the head of the table with her arms raised, activating the ritual: her human disguise is splitting, dark leathery wings and horns tearing out of her. Above the portal, the Fury, a goddess of rage and madness, rises as a towering spectral woman of fire and smoke, half-transparent and growing more solid by the moment, reaching down for Felicia as her vessel.
The party bursts in to stop it: Lirael hurling dark magic at the corpses that anchor the trance, Brand in his power armor charging, and Sazed caught mid-step, frozen and stunned by the glabrezu Gynn, a huge four-armed demon looming over him with pincer claws.
Among the hanging dead are Turckle the dwarf barkeep and the elderly Valvier couple, Neer and Elk.

CAST
- Felicia Jr: a young adult drow woman with grey-blue skin, messy short white hair, long pointed ears and pale green eyes; here unclothed and bound on the altar as described above, so none of her usual outfit (no top, sash, skirt or belt) is worn.
- Fark: a succubus mid-transformation, slim and pale-skinned with long black hair, pointed ears and icy pale blue eyes, dark leathery wings and curling horns tearing out of her, in scant dark red clothing and choker necklaces.
