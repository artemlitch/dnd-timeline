THE MOMENT
Kynds, a small halfling Aundairian officer, stands on the Moonwatch's own deck with a squad of Aundairian hobgoblin soldiers fanned out around him, their crossbows levelled at the party. Beside him stands Beezel, the ship's halfling helmsman, charmed: glassy-eyed, smiling vacantly, standing with the enemy. Facing Kynds at the head of the stairs, Sazed holds his hands open and empty, trying to talk him down. At the hatch behind Sazed, Eloria leads Captain Killian up onto the deck, a prisoner with his hands bound, bruised, smirking at his rescuer. Lirael stands at Sazed's shoulder, ready to cast, his eyes on Kynds, who watches him back too closely for any trick. Half hidden in the shadow of the stern cabins, Screech crouches with a weapon ready for an ambush that never comes.
