THE MOMENT
On the stage a bullywug band, Smarmy Joe and the Wully Bugs, plays fifties rock and roll: a row of man-sized frog-men in matching sharp jackets and bow ties, one on upright bass, one on drums, Smarmy Joe at the microphone with his guitar, beer bottles at their webbed feet. Waiting at the side of the stage is the next act, a kobold soul band, Shimmy Fistor and the Squabble Crowd: small red scaly dragon-like kobolds in wide-lapelled seventies suits, flared trousers and gold chains.
In the foreground Lirael sits at a table with Sheldor, the drow, who is leaning in and telling him about the far-off jungle continent of Xen'drik, hands sketching its size in the air. Grinning deep gnomes and duergar crowd the table, and one bald, white-bearded grey duergar is pressing a gift into Lirael's hands: a dark hood and face mask to shield a drow's eyes on sunny days. Sazed stands at the back of the room watching the band, a drink in hand.
