THE MOMENT
The party has leapt down from the Moonwatch onto the roof of the speeding carriage, together with their ally Mustang, a warforged police officer, and Felicia Jr. The Even Hand, a masked assassin, was waiting for them on the roof. Now Mustang turns on the party: he has been one of The Even Hand's bodies all along. On the swaying roof, Mustang swings his revolver point-blank into Brand's face and fires, muzzle flash blazing, while The Even Hand lunges in beside him with a blade, the two of them side by side as one. Brand staggers back in his power armor toward the roof's edge. Sazed, mid-leap, kicks at The Even Hand. Felicia Jr is fading into invisibility as she drops through the roof hatch, half of her already transparent. Grappling hooks and ropes trail from the roof edges where the enemies keep climbing back on. Beside the carriage a plague-masked rider is shot and tumbling from his saddle in a spray of blood.
