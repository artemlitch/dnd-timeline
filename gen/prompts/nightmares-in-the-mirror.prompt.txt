THE MOMENT
The tarp has slid off the tall mirror, and its glass shows three nightmares at once instead of reflections: a family walking away down a long pier, blood-red streets of Sharn full of smoke and marching soldiers in mechanical armor, and a burning shifter village ringed by soldiers. In front of it Lirael, Brand and Sazed reel back, sweating and stricken, each staring at his own nightmare. Sazed is already swinging his quarterstaff to smash the glass.
