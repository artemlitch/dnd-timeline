THE MOMENT
Major Blazehelm leads the party into the hangar, and Screech, a halfling pilot, hops down off the nose of his Shuriken, wiping grease off his hands, to meet them, grinning. Brand has already wandered off to the nearest fighter and is peering into its open engine panel, fascinated. Sazed hangs back, uneasy at being on a ship at all. Lirael shakes Screech's hand.
