THE MOMENT
Lirael is locked in a rusted cage, possessed, and this prison is inside his own mind. Feeling a little of his magic come back to him, he crouches at the cage door picking the lock with a jagged scrap of rusted metal, his jaw set. In the cages beside his sit his mother Felicia Sr, Xera and Fey-Lynn, slumped and staring at nothing. Floating just outside the bars is a beholder of the Dreamweaver, a dark, many-eyed horror with writhing tentacle eyestalks, each ending in a pale glowing eye, and one great central eye. It taunts him, and in the surface of its central eye, like a moving picture, it shows him Ellis's brutal death: the mercenary captain lying torn open and bloody in an Ardev street.
