{tone}
This scene is grief in the aftermath of a massacre: two men mourning their dead in a yard full of corpses.

FRAMING
{framing:wide}

SETTING
{setting:darkwood-prison/prison-yard}
In this moment: the battle against the necromancer's undead army has just ended, and the prison has not exploded yet: the cellblock still stands, burning, flames pouring from its windows and thick smoke rolling over the walls. The yard is churned mud and blood, heaped with the bodies of the risen dead, cut down for the last time: guards, prisoners and gang members in rotting prison fatigues and uniforms, some with smashed skulls. On the smoking roof above, stranded guards and prisoners wave for help. Time and light: dusk, the yard lit orange by the burning prison.
{maps:Prison Exterior 10ft squares}

THE MOMENT
In the quiet after the fight, two men kneel among the corpses. In the foreground Sorenson kneels in the mud over the body of Shmem Gorgis, his friend and tuba player. Shmem died in the riot, his body was burned in a hasty send-off, and then the necromancer raised him as a zombie that the party had to put down: his corpse is scorched, grey and rotting, with a fatal wound to the head. Sorenson holds Shmem's charred hand, head bowed, and Shmem's dented brass tuba lies in the mud beside them. A few steps away Leff, the huge half-orc, kneels over the zombified body of Rex, the gang leader he looked up to, gently closing his eyes with one enormous hand. Behind them, Lirael, Brand and Sazed stand bloodied and exhausted, catching their breath and watching in silence. Near the wall lies the body of the zombie Rolick.
