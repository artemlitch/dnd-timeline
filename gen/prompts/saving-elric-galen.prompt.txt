In this moment: a corridor between the cells, the riot alarm sounding, dead guards and prisoners lying where they fell, cell doors hanging open. Time and light: late afternoon, dim lamplight.

THE MOMENT
Elric Galen, a decent guard, is down on his back on the stone floor with blood on his face, his mace knocked out of reach, while another guard kneels on his chest and raises a short sword to drive it into his throat. In that instant the escaped prisoners arrive: Sazed, in grey prison fatigues, swings his quarterstaff into the side of the attacker's head, the skull cracking and blood spraying, the blade flying from his hand. Lirael, a young drow also in prison fatigues, has a hand raised, mid-spell. Brand, a stocky old dwarf in prison fatigues with no armor yet, charges in behind. Byll, a captured guard with his hands bound, hangs back against the bars, watching.
