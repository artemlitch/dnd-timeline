{maps:Wroat Street}

THE MOMENT
On the cobbles out front Kal Valvier, a young Ardev man, lies propped in Sazed's arms, a crossbow bolt through his body and blood soaking his shirt, just brought back from death by Sazed and pointing up at the roof with a shaking hand. Up on the roof edge The Even Hand crouches with his crossbow in the drifting smoke, and at that moment Brand's thorn whip, a long vine bristling with thorns, lashes around him and rips him off the roof. Lirael appears on the rooftop in a puff of silvery mist from his misty step, a second too late to grab him.
