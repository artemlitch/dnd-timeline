In this moment: the edge of the village among the huts. Time and light: morning, sun through the trees.
{maps:Demon Village}

THE MOMENT
Behind a hut, an imp is yanked off its feet and silenced by a strike from nowhere: only a faint shimmering outline of a man, Sazed, invisible, glowing with the soft golden light of a blessing. A cambion beside him crumples to its knees, struck by attackers no one can see, two more faint shimmers with the same golden glow. A few steps away, quasits keep picking through stolen crates, unaware.

CAST
- {cast:Sazed} Here: invisible, only a faint shimmering outline with a soft golden glow.
- The imp: tiny red devils with bat wings, small horns and stinging tails.
- The cambion: tall half-demons with dark red skin, curling horns and leathery wings, in scavenged armour.
- The quasits: small wiry green demons the size of a cat, with warty skin, little horns, clawed hands and long barbed tails.
