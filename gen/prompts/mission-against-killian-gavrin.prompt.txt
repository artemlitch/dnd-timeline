THE MOMENT
Major Blazehelm leans over the map table, one finger on the valley where the two Aundairian ships sit, explaining that the officer hunting them, Captain Killian Gavrin, thinks Blazehelm is his archrival, and that taking or wrecking both ships is now their job. Screech, still in his flight helmet and goggles, windblown from his scouting flight in his Shuriken, points out the staging area he spotted. On the table in front of Brand lie two heavy canvas satchels of smokepowder with fuses coiled on top, and Brand is lifting one, weighing it with interest. Lirael and Sazed study the two models: the light, fast Broadsword and the heavy, cannon-studded Broadaxe.
