THE MOMENT
Brand's squad finishes together. At the top of the tower, flags snatched in hand, Tauren and Leff have just leapt off the edge and hang in mid-air above the bushes, arms flailing. Right behind them on the tower's edge Brand, in his power armor, is bending his knees to jump, with Keys and Sorenson crowding at his shoulders ready to follow. Sorenson is mud-streaked and bruised from being knocked flat by the rolling spike log on the way. Below, Gunnery Sergeant Dyll Strickland stands at the foot of the tower bellowing insults up at them, red-faced, pointing, yet clearly approving. At the finish table nearby, Lirael and Sazed, who finished long ago, sit eating their breakfast and watching.
