In this moment: the ship is parked at the Ardev landing field. Time and light: mid-morning, candlelight and a shaft of daylight from the hatch above.
{maps:Below Deck Moonwatch}

THE MOMENT
Eloria, the ship's cleric, stands at the altar holding a solemn service for the ship's two animals: Rufus, a scruffy dog, sits on a crate as if listening, and Mr. Beaks, the ship's little bird, perches beside his treasured pile of cheese. A female bird has just flown in through the hatch and landed by him, and Mr. Beaks has bowed low before her and is pushing pieces of cheese from his pile toward her. Skye, the young shifter girl, crouched nearby, has just said it is cute, and at that the female bird has turned her head to stare straight at Skye and is speaking to her in a clear human voice, delivering a message. Skye stares back, stunned. Sazed, at her side, tenses as he listens. Behind them Brand, in disguise for the town, has been painted entirely purple, his skin, his beard and his hair, and wears a battered cowboy hat; he is narrowing his eyes, recognising the spell. Eloria has stopped mid-blessing.
