THE MOMENT
The party staggers down the gangway after a day of horrors to find two people waiting with their arms crossed. Barry Baritone, a big brass construct whose body is built from brass instruments, and Brokk Broadhelm, a red-bearded Cannith dwarf with a crackling brass gauntlet, are furious: Sorenson missed his recording session with Barry for the opening of the House Cannith studio, and now Thaddeus Cannith has had to push his speech back a day. Brokk jabs a finger up at Sorenson; Barry looms, orange eyes glowing. Sorenson stands halfway down the gangway, sheepish, mandolin on his back, shaking his head: he cannot record now. Behind him Lirael, Brand and Sazed look on, worn out and bloodstained. At the same moment, at the side of the gangway, Brand plants a boot in the back of Filgar, a scruffy rat-man, and shoves him off the ship onto the ground, where he scrambles away toward town. Along the ship's rail above, a group of rescued shifter children peer down, with Skye among them.
