THE MOMENT
Lirael's mother, Felicia Sr, has just come aboard and is bonding with the crew, sitting among them on a crate, a cigarette between her fingers and smoke curling around her. Sorenson stands before them with his mandolin, playing a new song that came to him in a dream. He has reached its opening line, a phrase Lirael's missing father always used to say, and Lirael has gone rigid, staring at Sorenson in shock, a coffee cup frozen halfway to his mouth. Felicia Sr has stopped mid-drag and turned to look at her son. Around them the rest of the crew listen happily, unaware: Screech the halfling pilot tapping his foot, Beezel grinning, Erald the old dwarf scribe nodding along.
