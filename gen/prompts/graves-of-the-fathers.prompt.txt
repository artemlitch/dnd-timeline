THE MOMENT
Lirael, Brand and Sazed have stopped at two graves side by side. Lirael crouches and brushes dust from one headstone, the grave of a High Marshal who fought in the ranks, the father of their old prison enemy Thaldamir. Brand, in his power armor, stands at the next stone, the grave of Gelf's father, his helmet lowered. Sazed holds the light high over both, and behind them the great ribs and skull of the dead dragon rise into the dark.
