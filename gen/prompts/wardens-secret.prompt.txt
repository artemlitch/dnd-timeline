THE MOMENT
Warden Sharpe is backed against the wall behind his desk, hands half raised, sweating, spilling everything: his prison is an experiment turning ex-Brelish soldiers into zombies for an attack on Karrnath. Lirael leans across the desk toward him, pressing him for more. Sazed blocks the door. Brand, still in his prison clothes and without his armor, holds the warden's secretary, Magnot Whitlin, by the collar; the thin little man clutches a stack of files to his chest, terrified.
