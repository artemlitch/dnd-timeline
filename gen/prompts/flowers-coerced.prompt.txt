THE MOMENT
Captain Flowers sits behind his desk while The Right Hand, the House Cannith emissary, leans over it with both hands planted on the desktop, finishing his order: the curfew will continue whether the townsfolk like it or not. Brand and Sazed stand behind Lirael on the landing, and Jazz hangs back at the top of the stairs.
