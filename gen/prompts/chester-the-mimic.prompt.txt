THE MOMENT
Lirael kneels before a heavy locked chest with his thieves' tools in the lock, and the lock has just clicked open. At that instant the chest comes alive: it is Chester the mimic. The lid splits into a grinning mouth of teeth, a long tongue lolls out, and a single big eye blinks at them, pleased and impressed. With its tongue it holds out a plain cloth sack as a reward. Lirael rocks back on his heels; Sazed, beside him, stares at the sack; Brand, in his power armor, has his gauntlet half raised.
