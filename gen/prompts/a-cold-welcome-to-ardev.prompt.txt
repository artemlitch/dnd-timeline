Brand and Lirael stand behind Sazed, braced for a fight. Then two of Ardev's police push through the ring and berate the mercenaries: Jazz, jabbing a finger in the lead mercenary's face, and Mustang, the warforged police officer, calmly stepping between the mercenaries' weapons and the party.
