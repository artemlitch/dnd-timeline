THE MOMENT
Lirael, Brand and Sazed step off the glowing teleportation circle into the tomb, having duplicated Paxon's portrait to slip through his secret teleporter. They stand at the edge of the graves, staring out at the rows of soldiers' headstones circling the great dragon skeleton, the dead of the Dragons of Centarrel and the Brelish soldiers who killed them, all buried in the same week the walls of Wroat fell. Sazed crouches by one headstone, brushing dust off it; Brand raises a hand toward the dragon bones in awe.
