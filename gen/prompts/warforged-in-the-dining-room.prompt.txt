THE MOMENT
The fight is won. Two Warforged Enforcers lie broken on the floor, and the hulking Clockwork Defender is down on one knee, sparking. Sazed stands over the Defender with one hand plunged into its open chest, ripping out a glowing carved rune, the construct's heart, trailing torn wires. He holds it up for Brand to see. Brand, in his power armor, plaster dust on his shoulders from bursting through the hidden door, stares at the rune and recognises the maker's work: this was built by Clockwork, the base's artificer, and the whole house is a test. Each fallen construct has a stabilizing syringe fitted into one hand. Behind them Lirael hurls a fire bolt at the Warforged Spellcaster, which has just flickered out of invisibility in a corner, half-transparent, raising its staff. Through the hole in the wall the friends fight on in the bedroom: Keys in the shape of a big black bear mauling another enforcer, Leff and Tauren punching at it.
