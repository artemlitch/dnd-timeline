THE MOMENT
Lirael holds open the Royal Ardev Regiment file Paxon just handed him: a long roster of names and addresses, the writing not legible. His face goes pale and stricken as he runs a finger down the list and recognises nearly every name: his neighbours from Ardev, conscripted. Paxon leans over his shoulder in his blue officer's coat, pointing out three entries on the page, the three men still living in Wroat: the blacksmith, Lirael's old literature teacher, and his high school friend.
