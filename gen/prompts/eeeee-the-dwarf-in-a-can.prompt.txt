THE MOMENT
The brass can Brand just hurled has landed among a squad of hobgoblin soldiers, burst open, and out has sprung EEEEE, the dwarf in a can, arms flung wide, grinning madly, shouting that he loves to kill and asking who he should kill. On a rise behind the hobgoblins an archer draws a green flare arrow.
