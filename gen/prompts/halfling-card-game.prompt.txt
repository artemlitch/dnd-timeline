THE MOMENT
Beezel has spent the night mocking Screech for dreaming of captaining his own ship, and Screech beat him by cheating: a spare card is still tucked half out of Screech's sleeve. Now Lirael has sat down against the two of them, and he lays down his winning hand with a straight face. Screech and Beezel stare at the cards, stunned, as Lirael pulls the whole pot of coins across the table. The other halfling pilots crowd around, laughing at them both.
