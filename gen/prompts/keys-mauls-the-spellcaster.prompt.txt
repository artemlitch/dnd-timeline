THE MOMENT
Keys, wild-shaped into a huge black bear, has the Sentinel spellcaster Lug Murzog pinned on the floor and is mauling him to death, jaws clamped on his throat and shoulder, claws tearing through his black Sentinel uniform, blood spraying across the floorboards. Lirael has just burst through the doorway, one hand raised and glowing as he casts a spell on Sheldor. Sheldor, the possessed drow, stands at bay in the middle of the room with a blade drawn, his face blank and strange. Brand, just pulled back out of the bag and freshly clamped into his power armor, swings an armored fist at the second Sentinel, Charlie Trundle. Sazed, in nightclothes, fights barefoot beside him. Crowding in behind them, half-dressed from sleep, come their friends: the huge tattooed half-orc Leff, Tauren with his greataxe, the red-bearded dwarf Sorenson, a drunk Cromwell, and a scruffy skinny dog barking.
