THE MOMENT
The Aundairian spies' carriage, piled high with newly bought weapons and armor, barrels straight across the square, smashing over the tables: tables flip, mugs and plates fly, and drinkers dive out of its path. Captain Killian drives, standing at the reins and whipping the horses toward the mouth of a narrow alley. Kynds clings to the crates on top, Darvok holds on at the back, and EEEEE rides in his brass can wedged among the armor. Chasing them on foot: Sazed sprints along the edge of the square, cutting ahead toward the alley; Lirael runs behind with one hand already raised to cast; Brand, a goblin in power armor, pounds after them.
