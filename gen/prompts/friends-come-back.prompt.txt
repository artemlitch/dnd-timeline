THE MOMENT
The freed Sentinels are sitting up on the couches, themselves again. In the centre, Eloria the cleric examines them with careful, old-school skill; she has handed Sazed an elegant silver stethoscope, and Sazed, its earpieces in, listens to the chest of Screech, a small halfling pilot, double-checking her work while she watches, impressed; Screech fidgets under the stethoscope, desperate to go and fly. Beside them Gelf, still groggy, stabs a finger at Brand, insisting he would have won a fair fight, and Brand, back in his power armor, glares back. Elric stands between them with his hands up. Charlie Trundle, a black-bearded Sentinel, shakes Lirael's hand, introducing himself for the first time. Sheldor, a drow, is already pulling on his coat to leave for Xen'drik.
