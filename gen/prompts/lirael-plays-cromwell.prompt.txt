SETTING
{setting:darkwood-prison/cells-and-bunks}
In this moment: Lirael's cell. The riot has not started yet; the cellblock is quiet and intact. A loose rock has been pulled out of the cell wall, and in the small cavity it left a crumpled little picture of a face is stuck to the stone: a bearded man in glasses with his mouth gaping wide open in exaggerated excitement. Time and light: the middle of the night, dim lamplight from the corridor falling through the bars.
{maps:Prison Level 1}

THE MOMENT
Lirael, in drab prison fatigues, sits hunched on the lower bunk holding the loose rock from the wall up close to his mouth: the rough little stone is a sending stone, and a stranger's voice is coming through it. He has just been asked for someone called Cromwell, and he is pretending to be him: chin tucked, face screwed up as he puts on a deeper, gruffer voice, one eye on the cell door. His bunkmates Tauren and the elf Elarian lean in from the bunk above and the shadows, wide-eyed, trying not to laugh or make a sound.
