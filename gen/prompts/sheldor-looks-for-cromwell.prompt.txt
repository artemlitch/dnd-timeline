SETTING
{setting:darkwood-prison/gate-and-walls}
In this moment: the prison has not exploded yet. Its entrance belches flame and black smoke, the cellblock behind it burning floor by floor, and destroyed zombies lie heaped on the ground. Near the gate the elf Thaldamir lies on his back with a broken leg, his face wet with spit. Freed survivors are drifting down off the smoking roof on a feather fall spell. Time and light: evening, dark, lit by the fire of the burning prison.
{maps:Prison Gate}

THE MOMENT
Sheldor Higgins has just come charging out of the flaming prison entrance, cutting down the last zombies in his way, a ragged column of freed prisoners stumbling out of the smoke behind him. He stops short, scanning the faces, shouting for his friend Cromwell. Lirael steps up to him, sheepish, one hand raised, admitting it was him on the sending stone all along. Sheldor glares at him, unhappy and betrayed. Behind Lirael, Brand in his power armor and Sazed in torn prison fatigues watch, exhausted and filthy from the battle.
