THE MOMENT
Before the closed dragon skull stands a row of clockwork wyrmlings: small mechanical dragons of tarnished brass and bronze, with riveted plates, exposed gears and leathery metal-ribbed wings, crouched motionless on guard, their eyes faintly lit. Lirael, Brand and Sazed stop at the edge of the light and look at them, deciding whether to push on. Sazed holds out an arm to keep the others back, Brand peers at the wyrmlings' mechanisms with professional interest, and Lirael glances back the way they came, toward the secret passage up to the Hobbstweedle cemetery.

CAST
- Clockwork wyrmling: a small dragon built entirely of brass and bronze machinery, gears and pistons visible, metal wings, about the size of a large dog.
- Lirael: a 23-year-old male drow sorcerer, slender, dark grey-blue skin, short white hair, black Sentinel coat.
- Brand: a 150-year-old male dwarf, short, stocky and broad, long grey-white beard and wild grey hair, in a brass-and-steel power armor suit with a glowing holy core.
- Sazed: a lean male shifter monk of about 45, very hairy but with an ordinary human face and normal human ears, wild swept-back dark reddish-brown hair, heavy sideburns and a rough beard, small round spectacles, in a black covert ops suit, carrying a quarterstaff.
