THE MOMENT
Dandy, in his apron, blasts a fire bolt into a small combustion engine Brand has bolted onto his tea machine in place of its hand crank. The machine's big dial swings all the way to its red maximum. Brand, in his power armor, and Dandy each hold a steaming teacup to their lips, and around their heads float small ghostly squirrels, jeering at them. Both men look deeply offended.
