THE MOMENT
Major Blazehelm stands at the head of the camp table, smiling, telling the three recruits that their imprisonment in DarkWood was arranged and that tearing the prison apart was their Sentinel trial all along; they did an amazing job. Lirael, Brand and Sazed, still filthy and battered from the prison and the escape, stare back at him across the table, realising it was all planned: even their fellow inmate and the one good guard were Sentinels placed there to watch over them.
