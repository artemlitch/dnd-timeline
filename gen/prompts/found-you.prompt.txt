SETTING

In this moment: the three nightmares are coming apart around the dreamers, fragments of each drifting in the dark: a burning village of huts by a river, a man trapped behind the glass of a mirror in a living room, and a dwarven forge doorway. Time and light: the dark of the dream, lit by the burning fragments and the eye.

THE MOMENT
Sazed, Lirael and Brand stand together in the dark at the end of their separate nightmares, each still shaken from his own: Sazed from his burning village, Lirael from his father trapped behind the mirror, Brand from the forge that no longer knows him. Above them the darkness opens into a single giant eye, filling the sky, its pupil fixed on the three of them, and it speaks two words that boom through the dream: "Found you." All three stare up at it, frozen.
