THE MOMENT
Lirael has just told Xera that Turckle is dead, and Neer and Elk with him, killed by demons who wanted to use his sister as a vessel for the Fury. Xera, hastily dressed after being woken at her window, stands stricken, one hand at her mouth. With the other she holds out her pendant necklace to Lirael, the one that came to her in an unaddressed letter a year ago, which he has told her is dangerous. Lirael, still in his coat and breathless from the climb, reaches for it, his face grave.
