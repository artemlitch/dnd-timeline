THE MOMENT
Major Blazehelm leans across the feast table toward Lirael and tells him how he knew Lirael was possessed when he killed the head of the magic society: a young wizard saw it and came to him, and Lirael's own father came to him before he vanished searching for the Shadow Mirror. Lirael, the young drow, grips the stem of his wine glass so hard his knuckles pale, his face caught between shock and hope at hearing his father spoken of, the noise of the party carrying on all around them.
