This scene is a grim burial in a burned village interrupted by a living tree.

FRAMING
{framing:wide}

SETTING
{setting:stones-throw/eldgrave-treant}
In this moment: the shifter village has been burned to the ground a week ago; its huts are charred ruins. A pile of burned corpses lies in the middle of the village, the bodies scorched with the white, glassy marks of radiant burns. Time and light: day.
{maps:Stones Throw}

THE MOMENT
The party is digging graves for the dead. Sazed stands over the corpse pile with a shovel, grief-stricken, these were his people. Brand, in his power armor, has just lifted a +1 greatsword out of a heap of fallen trees, and that heap is rising: it is Eldgrave the treant, unfolding to his full height out of what looked like a pile of logs, bark face opening its eyes and looking down at them, wary. Lirael steps back, hand raised to cast. A dented silver helm, the Helm of the Devout, lies in the ash at their feet.
