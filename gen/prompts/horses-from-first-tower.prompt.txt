SETTING
{setting:starilaskur/starilaskur-police-station}
In this moment: the yard behind the military police station, where the bombers' carriage stands with its two horses still in harness. In the back of the carriage lies the dead changeling bomber in a Starilaskur Military Police uniform, its face a smooth, pale, half-formed blank. Above the rooftops the shimmering defensive barrier the paladins raised still arches over the town. Time and light: evening, dark, lit by the lanterns of the station yard.
{maps:Starilaskur}

THE MOMENT
Beezel, a small halfling druid, stands on an upturned crate nose to nose with one of the carriage horses, speaking to it with animal magic and holding up an unfolded map of the region to its face, one finger on the route. The horse stares back blankly, chewing, plainly stupid. Sergeant Taggart, notebook in hand, leans in beside them, completely absorbed, working out from the horses' travel time where the bombers set out from. Lirael stands at Taggart's shoulder, watching the sergeant as much as the horse, while Brand in his power armor and Sazed wait by the carriage.
