This scene is the public unveiling of a prank in front of the whole base.

{framing:wide}

SETTING
{setting:blazehelms-mansion/main-hall}
In this moment: a crowd of Sentinels in black uniforms fills the main hall for the morning unveiling, gnome cooks in white aprons among them. On the wall hangs a large, formal oil portrait of Chief Petty Officer Paxon in his blue officer's coat, a perfect copy of the one from his room, except that it has been crudely drawn all over: scribbled-on additions deface his face and uniform. It has no words on it. A cloth that covered it has just fallen to the floor. Time and light: eight in the morning, daylight through the windows.
{maps:Mansion Main Floor}

THE MOMENT
The whole hall bursts out laughing at the defaced portrait. Major Blazehelm, at the front, laughs openly with a hand over his chest. The real Paxon stands right beneath his own portrait, rigid, humiliated and glaring at the crowd; he looks exactly like his character art (a pale-skinned elf with silver-white hair in a low ponytail, clean-shaven, in his royal-blue officer's coat), not like the defaced painting above him. Apart from the painting, Paxon appears only once. Off to one side, Lirael, Brand and Sazed stand with straight, innocent faces. Gunnery Sergeant Dyll Strickland, beside them, eyes the three of them with a knowing, approving half-smile. Spingus, the head chef gnome, holds out his palm to Lirael, who is dropping a small coin purse into it.
