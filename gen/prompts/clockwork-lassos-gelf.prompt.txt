This scene is a rescue by rope out of a magic bag, ending in a brawl.

FRAMING
{framing:wide}

SETTING
{setting:blazehelms-mansion/war-rooms}
In this moment: the upstairs war room, the map table shoved aside. On the floor lies the bag of devouring, its mouth stretched open on a dark void and the drifting silver-black of the Astral Sea. Time and light: lamplight.
{maps:Mansion Top Floor}

THE MOMENT
Everyone hauls on one long rope of several ropes knotted together, which runs across the floor and down into the mouth of the bag. Brand, in his power armor, anchors the end, heels dug into the floorboards. Clockwork, a tall warforged who cannot dream and so cannot be possessed, is climbing out of the bag's mouth, dragging the rope's lasso end, and in its loop comes Gelf, pulled up out of the void. Gelf is still possessed by the Dreamweaver: the moment his head clears the bag he is already swinging a fist at the nearest person, eyes blank, while Lirael and Sazed drop the rope and lunge to knock him out.
