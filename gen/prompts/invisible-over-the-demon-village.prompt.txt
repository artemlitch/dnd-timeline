SETTING

In this moment: a ridge of ferns and pines above the hidden village of small demon huts, smoke rising among the trees. Time and light: dusk, the last light through the forest.
{maps:Demon Village}

THE MOMENT
Sazed, Lirael and Brand crouch in the ferns on the ridge, each downing a potion of invisibility: Sazed is already fading to a half-transparent shimmer, Lirael is halfway gone, and Brand, in his power armor, is lifting the bottle to his lips. Below them the village is busy: imps flitting between huts, quasits picking through stolen crates, and cambions lounging by a fire.
