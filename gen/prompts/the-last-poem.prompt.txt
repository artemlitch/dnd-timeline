THE MOMENT
Lirael holds his father's journal open at its last page, a short poem. In front of him, on the desk, the black rune-carved obelisk has just lit up: it pours out a strange black light that throws everything into negative, and where that light falls on the wall a hidden door outline glows into view. Brand, in his power armor, and Sazed step back, staring at the door.
