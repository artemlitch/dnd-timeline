THE MOMENT
The memory Lirael has finally let himself see: the Watchers of the Gate, robed warlocks, stand in a circle around their Grand Warlock Maldroc as the ritual peaks, and every mirror on the walls has become a doorway into the silvery astral sea. In the middle of the circle Lirael, possessed by the Dreamweaver, his eyes gone black and empty, is tearing the Watchers apart with his bare magic: bodies ripped open, blood and gore sprayed across the mirrors and the floor, robed figures falling around him, and the shelves of the library behind him catching fire.
