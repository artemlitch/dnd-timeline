THE MOMENT
Each recruit sits in a chair while an instructor uses a spell on them and asks questions they must not answer. In the centre, Lirael sits facing Elric, who has a hand raised, eyes glowing faintly with detect thoughts, while Eloria stands beside Elric helping with the spell. Lirael misunderstood the exercise and has just cheerfully blurted out the true answers to the first three secret questions. Gunnery Sergeant Dyll Strickland is bent right over him, nose to nose, red-faced and screaming. To one side Feldor Windrake, the airship captain, has cast confusion on Sazed, who sits swaying and blinking but tight-lipped; Brand sits stiffly inside a faint shimmering circle of light, a zone of truth, jaw clenched, giving nothing away. At the end of the row, Leff, the huge half-orc, sits with a perfectly blank, peaceful face, acing his questions by not thinking about anything at all.
