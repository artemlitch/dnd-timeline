THE MOMENT
Sheldor, the drow, with the rescue rope still knotted round his waist, stands over the open bag and jabs a finger down into it, adamant that he is going back in for the rest of the loot. His eyes are a little too bright and reckless. Across from him Leff, Keys and Tauren, still roped, shake their heads and step back from the bag, having felt a dark presence inside it. Lirael stands slightly apart, arms folded, studying Sheldor's face closely. Brand, in his power armor, plants himself between Sheldor and the bag. A drunk Cromwell leans in the stable doorway, and the red-bearded dwarf Sorenson holds the rope with a scruffy grey dog at his feet.
