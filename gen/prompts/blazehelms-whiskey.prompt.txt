THE MOMENT
Major Blazehelm stands at the head of the table and passes his personal whiskey down the line, one swig each before they head for the valley: Brand is mid-swig, Lirael is reaching for it next. In the foreground, at the end of the table, Screech has pulled Sazed aside and with a sly look opens his flight jacket a crack to show a small glass potion bottle hidden inside, an invisibility potion he stole.
