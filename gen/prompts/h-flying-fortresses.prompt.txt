THE MOMENT
A vast flying fortress, a whole military base of stone walls, towers and gun emplacements on a floating island of rock bound with glowing arcane rings, rises over the hills. Soldiers drill in ranks on its battlements, and a crowd of House Cannith artificers on the ground below watches it lift.

CAST
- The artificers: House Cannith artificers in work aprons and goggles.
- The soldiers: Brelish soldiers in dark blue uniforms.
