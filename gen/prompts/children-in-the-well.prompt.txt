SETTING
{setting:stones-throw/stones-throw-well}
In this moment: at the well. Nearby lies the heap of burned shifter corpses, men, women and elders, their flesh scorched by radiant fire. Time and light: about two in the morning, moonlight.
{maps:Stones Throw}

THE MOMENT
Eldgrave, a huge treant, has just heaved the heavy rock off the well's mouth and holds it in his branch-hands. Brand, in his power armor, is levering the last nailed boards off the plug with a pry bar. Sazed kneels at the edge, leaning down into the dark. Looking up at him from the hiding place below is Skye, a ten-year-old shifter girl, filthy, thin and fierce, shielding eight small children, aged two to five, huddled behind her in the dark where they have hidden for a week. A rope lies coiled ready beside Sazed.
