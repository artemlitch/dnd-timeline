This scene is a race to escape a dream as it falls apart.

SETTING
{setting:dreamweaver-dream/collapsing-platforms}
In this moment: the rock platforms are breaking up: chunks vanish underfoot, burst into blinding light, or float away into the dark. A glowing portal stands open on the last platform. Time and light: the dark of the dream, lit by the portal and bursting rocks.

THE MOMENT
A gigantic featureless face rises out of the dark below, its eyes gouged out by the dragon, a picture of agony, and its enormous hands smash down on the platforms and reach to grab the party and keep them in the collapsing dream. Lirael and Brand, in his power armor, stand at the portal, shouting and waving the others on. Sazed runs back across the crumbling rocks, dragging Gelf and Screech by the arms, with Cromwell leaping a gap behind them. And out of the dark, the long brass tail of a brass dragon wraps around the giant face, about to drag it and its hands down into the darkness.
