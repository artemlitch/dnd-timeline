THE MOMENT
Barry Baritone, the singing construct, has led the party to the forge. In the doorway they meet The Right Hand, Merrix d'Cannith's emissary and bodyguard, on his way out, Archimedes, his brass warforged owl, perched on his shoulder. He pauses to warn Brand that the party has angered the Dragons Claw mercenaries. At that moment Jeff, the frog-man, finally arrives, staggering up the street out of breath and furious after running all the way across town to answer the ring's summons. Through the open forge doors, inside at the glowing forge, Brokk, Brand's nephew, looks up from his anvil and glares at his uncle.
