THE MOMENT
Grail Grimdell's corpse has risen from the dirt: the blood-red gem of his necklace blazes, and he stands as an awakened zombie, grey and rotting, arms flung wide, laughing like a madman, declaring that they have only given him his final form. Around him every fallen zombie is clawing back up to its feet, their dead faces now full of the people they used to be, among them Rolick, a former friend, now a zombie. Lirael, Brand in his power armor and Sazed, exhausted, scramble back up to fight.
