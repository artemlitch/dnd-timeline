THE MOMENT
In two days Lirael has lost the librarians Meriel and Mercy, the blacksmith Murphy Gravewood and his school friend Kal, both from his home village of Ardev. He sits at Eloria's piano playing a traditional Ardev mourning song, head bowed, eyes wet, his hands heavy on the keys. Eloria has sat down on the bench beside him and joined in on the upper keys without a word, her shoulder against his, watching him with quiet sympathy. Their hands share the keyboard in the foreground.
