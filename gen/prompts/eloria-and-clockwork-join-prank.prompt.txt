THE MOMENT
The party is recruiting help to humiliate Paxon, the fussy chief petty officer everyone on base is tired of: they will copy his big self-portrait, draw all over the copy and hang it in the mansion's main hall. Eloria, the base's senior cleric, has just been asked to forge the paperwork and supply a scroll of facsimile, and she is thrilled to be included: grinning, leaning over the bench, already filling in a forged form with a flourish while holding up the rolled scroll of facsimile in her other hand. Beside her, Clockwork, the warforged artificer, holds out a large pot of industrial glue with a brush standing in it, his skull-like face impassive but his posture pleased and upright, a willing accomplice. Lirael and Brand lean in across the bench, and Sazed keeps watch at the shop door.
