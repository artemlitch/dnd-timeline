THE MOMENT
Cromwell appears in a puff of silver mist on the train roof and shoves a Flock soldier off the edge. Below, the car's doors stand open with more of the Flock crowding in. Far off in the sky, the Moonwatch blinks an urgent light signal, and Sazed, climbing out onto the roof, turns to read it.
