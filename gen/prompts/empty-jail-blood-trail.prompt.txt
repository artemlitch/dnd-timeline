In this moment: the cell doors hang open and the cells are empty. Two of the major's personal guards lie dead on the floor in spreading blood, and bloody footprints lead out of the jail door. The village alarm is blaring. Time and light: the small hours before dawn, lamplight inside the jail.
{maps:Hobbstweedle Jail}

THE MOMENT
The party has just arrived with their prisoners and found the cells empty. Sazed crouches over the bloody footprints at the doorway, studying the trail that leads out north into the night. Clockwork, the warforged artificer, stands among the bodies with one hand raised, having just switched the village alarm back on with a thought. Lirael holds his sending stone up to his mouth, waking Major Blazehelm with the news. Cromwell stands at the open cell doors with an arrow nocked. Behind them, two warforged enforcers carry the party's unconscious, possessed prisoners slung over their shoulders: Brand limp in his power armor, and the halfling pilot Screech. The hulking Clockwork Defender fills the doorway.
