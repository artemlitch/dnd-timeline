THE MOMENT
Elric, mid-conversation, has suddenly realised someone has been inside his head all night and worked out it was Lirael. Half-risen from his chair, red-faced and scolding, he has just flung a spoon across the table: it is in mid-air, an instant from bouncing off Lirael's forehead. Lirael, caught, flinches back in his chair; he is still in drab grey prison fatigues, not his usual black coat. Sazed, now in his new black covert ops uniform, and Brand, in his power armor, look on from either side of the table.
