Time and light: midday, daylight through the workshop windows and lamps over the benches.

THE MOMENT
Faird, an old veteran who can no longer fight, has just rolled in through the workshop door in a wooden wheelchair and asked if they can do anything to get him fighting again. Brand, in his power armor, and Clockwork, the warforged artificer, stand at the bench with their tools still in hand. Both of them look down at the warforged arm on the bench, then slowly turn to look at each other, the same idea dawning on both of them, while Faird watches them hopefully.
