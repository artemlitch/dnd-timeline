THE MOMENT
Lirael and Cromwell ease the war room door open, crouched low, creeping in to steal Paxon's copy of the sewer map, and freeze mid-step. Paxon is right there at his desk, already bent over a large sheet, carefully inking a fresh copy of the sewer map with a fine quill beside the original: a sprawling drawing of branching tunnels running beneath the village houses down to the river, with no words on it. He glances up at the two intruders over the top of the sheet, unimpressed and unsurprised, quill still in hand.
