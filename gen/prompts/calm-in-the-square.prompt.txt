THE MOMENT
Lirael stands in the middle of the square, hands raised, a soft wave of calm spreading from him. Around him a brawl is freezing mid-swing: Morganth lowers his weapon, Auntie Bees stops, and the townsfolk relax. Only The Right Hand still glares at Lirael, resentful. Xera dances near the stage. At the edge of the square, a great stag vanishes into a tree.
