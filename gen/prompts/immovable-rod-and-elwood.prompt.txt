THE MOMENT
The Aundairian spies' carriage, loaded with newly bought weapons and armor, is stuck dead in the alley: a short iron rod hangs fixed in mid-air against its back, immovable, and the horses strain uselessly. Lirael's hypnotic pattern still blazes over the alley, and it is a central, prominent part of the image: a shimmering, glowing illusion of a whole troupe of dwarves in lingerie, dancing in a swirling kaleidoscope of light above and around the carriage. The other Aundairian spies stand frozen beneath it, charmed and slack-jawed, staring up at the dancing dwarves. The dwarf EEEEE lies dead on the cobbles beside his brass can, killed by Sazed a moment ago. Now Captain Killian leaps down from the carriage and swings his brand-new battle axe into Sazed: the blade bites deep into Sazed's side and shoulder in a spray of blood, half his strength gone in a single cleave, Sazed staggering but still fighting. Lirael stands further back in the alley hurling a bolt of fire at Killian. And bounding into the alley comes Jeff, a huge frog-man, mouth gaping impossibly wide, about to swallow one of the Aundairians whole.
