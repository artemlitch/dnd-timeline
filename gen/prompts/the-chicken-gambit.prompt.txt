THE MOMENT
The succubus Fark is dead and the portal has swallowed her, but it has spat out two demon women and the Fury still rises above it, a towering spectral woman of fire and smoke reaching down for her vessel. That vessel, Felicia Jr, cannot leave the portal's bounds in any form, so Lirael has turned her into a chicken. Now Lirael clutches the chicken, a plump, terrified brown hen flapping wildly in his arms, and misty-steps: he is half gone in a burst of silvery mist at the edge of the portal, the hen still in his arms, the Fury's huge hand closing on empty air. At that exact instant a blinding white flash is starting to bloom out of the mist. Around them, Brand in his power armor and Sazed with his quarterstaff fight Gynn, the huge four-armed glabrezu, while the two demon women raise their hands to cast.
