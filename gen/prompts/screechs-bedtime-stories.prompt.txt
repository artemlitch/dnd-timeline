SETTING
{setting:moonwatch/crew-cabins}
In this moment: Screech's small cabin below deck. Fey-Lynn has the bunk; on the floor beside it lies Screech's own bedroll, a thin blanket and a flattened pillow where he has been sleeping. The Moonwatch is parked on the Ardev landing field. Time and light: morning, daylight through a porthole and a small lamp still lit.
{maps:Below Deck Moonwatch}

THE MOMENT
The party, expecting the worst when Screech missed breakfast, has pushed open the cabin door, and finds this: Fey-Lynn, a drow woman, revived from death and still fragile, sits up in the bunk wrapped in a blanket, calmer than they have ever seen her, listening. Screech sits cross-legged on the floor beside her, arms spread wide, mid-way through telling her the story of how they stole the Moonwatch, one hand swooping like an airship leaping off a waterfall. In the doorway, Lirael, Brand and Sazed stop short.
