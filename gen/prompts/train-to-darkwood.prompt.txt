THE MOMENT
The train has lurched to a stop and the carriage door has just slid open. Blinding light floods in on the prisoners chained to the benches, all of them squinting and throwing up shackled hands against the glare. Lirael and Sazed sit side by side, waking, with the huge tattooed half-orc Leff and the wiry Rolick chained beside them. Silhouetted in the doorway, armed guards in Brelish uniforms wait to haul them out.
