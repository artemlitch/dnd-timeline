In this moment: the Moonwatch is in flight at night over Breland. The band is set up at one end of the deck. Time and light: night, lit by lanterns hung along the rail and the glow of the elemental ring at the stern.
{maps:Main Deck Moonwatch}

THE MOMENT
The ship's band plays traditional songs from Screech's home village of Canuck, from sheet music Screech brought: Screech, the new captain, pounds the drums, ecstatic, Sorenson plays his guitar and Dandy, the gnome cook, plays bass. The whole crew dances on the deck, arm in arm, stomping and spinning: Lirael, Brand and Sazed among them, Erald, the old dwarf scribe, and Cromwell, the stowaway, with a bottle. At the wheel behind them, Beezel, the halfling helmsman on the night shift, steers, tapping his foot.
