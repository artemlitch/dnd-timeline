Charlie Trundle, a captured Sentinel, sits tied to a post, bruised from the fight. He talks smoothly, playing along with Lirael's lie. Lirael crouches in front of him and stares into his eyes, reading his mind, and his face has gone cold: inside the man's head he can hear a second voice, not the man's own, feeding him what to say.
