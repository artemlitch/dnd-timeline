Brand, the old dwarf in his power armor, has gone completely still, a half-raised tankard forgotten in his armored hand, his eyes wet above his long grey-white beard after more than a hundred years of service and decades in prison. Blazehelm rests a steady hand on Brand's armored shoulder.
