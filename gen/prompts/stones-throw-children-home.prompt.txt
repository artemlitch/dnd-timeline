THE MOMENT
The eight small orphans of Stones Throw, two to five years old, are running and throwing themselves at Fang, the shifter guard, who has dropped to his knees to catch them all in his arms, near tears. He never expected to see them again. Sazed stands a little behind, having brought them here, with Skye beside him, watchful and guarded. Falken, the village elder, hurries up, overjoyed, reaching for Sazed. The watching villagers' suspicion is giving way to cautious hope.
