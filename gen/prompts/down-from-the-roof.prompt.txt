THE MOMENT
Lirael, arms raised, floats a line of survivors gently down from the burning roof with feather fall: guards and prisoners drifting down like leaves, and Chester the mimic, a treasure chest with a big eye and a lolling tongue, bobbing down among them. Thaldamir is the exception: Lirael has let him drop, and the elf lies on the ground clutching a broken leg, howling, as Brand, in his power armor, winds up to knock him out. Erald, the nervous clerk, waves up at Chester.
