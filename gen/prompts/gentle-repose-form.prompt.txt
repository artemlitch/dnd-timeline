THE MOMENT
The party has come to ask how to save their friend Kal, who was beheaded by an assassin the day before. Eloria sits across the table from Lirael, Brand and Sazed with a cup of tea. There are no diamonds left for a resurrection, so she slides an official military form across the table to them with one finger, eyebrows raised, telling them it is a form they definitely "shouldn't" fill out and definitely "shouldn't" take over Paxon's head. Lirael, haggard and grieving, stares at the form; Brand frowns at it; Sazed sips his coffee. Behind them, Eleanor, her roommate, is bent over the compressor on her workbench with a wrench, goggles down, paying them almost no attention.
