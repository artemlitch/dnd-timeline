This scene is the reveal of a hidden military camp: escaped prisoners stepping through an illusion into a secret world.

FRAMING
{framing:wide}

SETTING
{setting:yedani-mountains/hidden-clearing-camp}
In this moment: the air ripples like a curtain of water where the massive illusion ends. On the near side the clearing looks empty; past the threshold, a humongous brand-new Brelish war airship, the Kingsblade, fills the clearing, its gangway lowered and rows of military tents pitched beneath its hull, with soldiers in black uniforms moving between them. Time and light: about one in the morning, lit by lanterns among the tents and lights along the ship's hull.
{maps:Dead Wood Clearing}

THE MOMENT
Lirael, Brand and Sazed step through the rippling edge of the illusion and stop short, staring up at the colossal airship that was invisible a moment ago. Behind them come a ragged line of freed prisoners in drab prison fatigues. At the foot of the gangway Major Renaud Blazehelm, in plate armor and a red cloak, stands waiting to welcome them, one arm open toward the nearest tent. Beside him stands Chief Petty Officer Smildar Paxon with a clipboard sheet, looking over the unexpected number of new arrivals with open irritation.
