THE MOMENT
In the middle of the dance floor, Sazed, who tried to flirt with Eloria, has been pulled out to dance by her: she leads, laughing, her tail swishing, while Sazed concentrates hard on his feet, stiff and flustered but smiling. At the edge of the floor, Eleanor, Eloria's engineer roommate, who has no interest in dancing or in being at the bar at all, has just been helped off the floor by Lirael and is now hiding behind Brand at the bar, crouched low behind the stocky dwarf, peering out over his shoulder; Brand stands with his tankard, unbothered, acting as her wall. In the doorway, Gauss Hog, an old drow soldier, has just arrived wearing a brand-new black Sentinel uniform, raising a hand in greeting.
