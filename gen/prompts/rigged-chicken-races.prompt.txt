THE MOMENT
Live chickens sprint flapping and squawking down the lanes of the table, feathers flying, one bursting over the finish line ahead of the rest. The deep gnomes, small grey-skinned bald gnomes, lean over the track howling at their birds, and Screech and Keys pound the table urging on chickens that are clearly going to lose. Lirael, who rigged the whole thing, sits at the end of the table calmly raking a large pile of gold coins toward himself, nobody suspecting a thing.
