SETTING
{setting:blazehelms-mansion/main-hall}
In this moment: long feast tables run the length of the main hall, set for a five-course dinner with bottles and glasses everywhere, gnome cooks and butlers carrying in dishes. The hall is packed wall to wall with Sentinels in black uniforms and party clothes: humans, gnomes, deep gnomes, duergar, dwarves, dragonborn, halfling pilots and orcs. Bodyguards stand at the foot of the stairs to the upper floor. Time and light: evening, lit by the hall's chandeliers and candles.
{maps:Mansion Main Floor}

THE MOMENT
Major Blazehelm stands at the head of the hall, glass raised high, finishing a speech so rousing that the whole crowd is on its feet roaring back at him, glasses raised; a faint golden glow of vigor shimmers over everyone in the hall. At a table near the front, the three new recruits, already half drunk, pour their drinks out onto the floor for their dead friends from the prison.
