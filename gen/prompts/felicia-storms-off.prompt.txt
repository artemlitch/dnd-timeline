THE MOMENT
Lirael has finally tried to talk to his sister, and it has gone badly. Felicia Jr, in her barmaid's apron, has turned her back on him and is storming toward the door, her face streaked with angry tears, furious that he left her when she needed him. Lirael stands by a table, one hand half raised after her, stunned. Turckle, the dwarf barkeep, is hurrying after Felicia with an arm out to comfort her and take her home, throwing Lirael a sad look over his shoulder. Behind the bar, Xera watches it all, and Neebus, the parrot-folk cook, pokes his head out through the kitchen door to come and help her.
