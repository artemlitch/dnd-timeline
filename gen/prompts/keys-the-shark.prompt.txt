THE MOMENT
Keys has turned himself into a shark: a big grey reef shark tearing up the channel, dorsal fin and back cutting through the surface in a long wake, leaving everyone behind. Behind him Brand, in his heavy brass-and-steel power armor, trudges chest-deep through the water, grim and tense, a dwarf who has just developed a fear of water, swinging an armored fist at a dummy as he wades. The other recruits wade after him and hack at the dummies as they go: the drow Sheldor, the red-bearded dwarf Sorenson, the huge tattooed half-orc Leff, and Tauren with his greataxe held over his head.
