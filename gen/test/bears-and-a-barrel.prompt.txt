SETTING
{setting:even-hand-train/train-car-interior}
In this moment: the narrow car is wrecked, benches splintered, the headless bodies of the two murdered women lying in blood on the floor. Harsh lightning flickers past the windows. At one end the arcane safe stands open, its doorway a swirling silver void of the Astral Plane.
{maps:Inside Lightning Rail 1#2}

THE MOMENT
Moments after the hostages were killed, Sazed and Beezel's war bears charge The Even Hand and Mustang down the length of the car. Three huge brown bears smash through the benches. The Even Hand has driven his long blade deep into the throat of Maisha, the lead bear, and she is collapsing onto him in a gush of blood, dying. Mustang braces and fires his revolver point-blank into a second bear's shoulder. Sazed vaults over the dying bear with his quarterstaff swinging at The Even Hand's masked head. Beezel, a small halfling druid, rides on the back of the third bear, roaring them on.
And at that exact moment a wooden barrel comes tumbling out of the astral portal and bursts open on the floor, spilling out Cromwell, a grey-haired ranger, drunk, bleary and half-asleep, who stowed away in it and now scrambles for his bow to help.

CAST
- {cast:Sazed}
- The Even Hand: an assassin in a dark beaked plague-doctor mask and black tricorn hat, black and deep-red layered leather coat with a studded collar, armored forearms, a long blade. His bodies are warforged constructs under the clothes.
- Mustang: a warforged police officer, metal face with glowing blue eyes, brass and steel joints, navy blue peaked police cap and long navy double-breasted police greatcoat, red tie, brown boots, revolver in hand.
- Beezel: a small, scruffy halfling with tousled dark hair, pointed ears and a cheeky grin, in a hooded traveller's tunic and leather bracers.
- Cromwell: a weathered human ranger in his fifties with swept-back grey hair and a short grey beard, grey leather armor with pauldrons, longbow and quiver.
