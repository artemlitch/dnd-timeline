This scene is a huge, chaotic town-wide battle: a whole town rising together against a monster.

FRAMING
{framing:epic}

SETTING
{setting:ardev/ardev-abandoned-house}
In this moment: the abandoned house stands among the two-storey homes and streets of Ardev, and it has just been torn apart from below, timbers and stone flying, a crater where it stood. The streets around it are packed with fighters and the rooftops with onlookers. The lake and the farmland are visible beyond the town. Time and light: a grey winter day.
{maps:Ardev}

THE MOMENT
The whole of Ardev has come to fight the bone devil, enemies included, under a truce for one day. The bone devil, a towering, gaunt, skeletal fiend with bat-like wings and a barbed scorpion tail, has just burst up through the abandoned house, destroying it, and summoned a second bone devil beside it. Around them, from every side, the town attacks:
- The Bloodsinger has turned into a huge triceratops and charges the first devil head-on, horns lowered.
- Auntie Bees flies overhead, and a vast swirling cloud of a million bees pours from her down onto the devils like a golden storm.
- Two weresharks, half-shark brutes, tear at the second devil.
- The town police, led by their warforged officer, fire from behind an overturned cart; the Dragons Claw mercenaries charge in a wedge led by their dragonborn commander Morganth and the dwarf Brokk with his crackling Cannith gauntlet; druids in hides and antlers cast vines and lightning.
- The party is at the heart of it: Brand in his glowing power armor in the front line, Sazed leaping at a devil's tail with his quarterstaff, Lirael hurling dark magic from a rooftop.
- At the edge of the crowd, two reporters scribble notes.
Blood, splinters, smoke and bees fill the air.
